HOW TO READ THIS VIEW

One sector.
Different questions.

01

Sector total divides a sum by a matching sum. Real losses stay in aggregate earnings; nonpositive aggregate earnings is N/M, not zero.

02

Typical company is the median eligible company. For P/E that describes profitable companies only — a different question, not a better average.

03

Coverage changes the comparison. A shared chart context does not imply that P/E and growth share a constituent set — each axis exposes its own cohort.

04

Sector fundamentals do not measure an ETF's return. Its membership, weights, fees and concentration may all differ.
