# N1 — CRCL monitoring example

Design example recorded 2026-09-12. These are research topics and conditional
mechanisms, not a current investment verdict or a running monitor. CRCL identifies
Circle equity; USDC and Open USD identify stablecoin products, not shares.

## Resolved user reference

The user supplied [Reap's Open USD explainer](https://reap.global/blog/what-is-open-usd-stablecoin),
dated July 1, 2026, to identify **Open Standard's Open USD (OUSD) stablecoin**.
This resolves the earlier ambiguous term. Reap states it is a participant, so
its article should carry that interest when used as evidence. The confirmed topic
is distinct from Circle's USDC and unrelated uses of the name OpenUSD.

The operator's [June 30 announcement](https://joinopenstandard.com/blog/introducing-open-usd)
describes distributing reserve earnings to partners after an operating management
fee. That is evidence of the announced proposition, not a measured loss of Circle
customers or income. The [operator's partner directory](https://joinopenstandard.com/partners)
is another source to monitor; being listed does not establish exclusive routing,
a completed integration or a quantified commercial commitment.

## Seed topics and research questions

| Topic | What to collect and track | Conditional implication to assess for CRCL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open Standard / Open USD | Changes to published economics and terms; verified availability; partner integrations and routing commitments; comparable circulation/adoption observations when available; Circle's disclosures about distribution costs and customer retention. | If partners shift material activity from USDC or demand more of its economics, growth or retained earnings could face pressure. Expansion of the overall market or use of multiple stablecoins can offset substitution. Partner logos alone cannot decide between these mechanisms. |
| CLARITY legislation | Official bill/action identity, each relevant text/amendment, hearings, implementation dates and a cited provision-to-business relationship. | A provision could change distribution access, compliance costs or competitive conditions; assess the actual text and stage. A positive headline about digital assets is insufficient to assign a benefit to Circle. Keep any related stablecoin legislation under its own identity. |
| Circle / Arc network | Official milestones, independently observable production activity where available, outages, integrations, usage incentives, costs and disclosed economics attributable to Circle. | Adoption could support demand for Circle services and USDC, but investment costs, incentives, execution risk and unclear revenue capture may offset it. Testnet traffic, partner count, token activity and CRCL shareholder earnings are distinct. |
| USDC business drivers and distribution | Dated circulation and reserve disclosures; period averages where reported; reserve yields; distribution agreements/costs; corporate versus customer reserve balances; sources for each measurement. | Determine whether a topic changes balances, earned yields, distribution expense or other business income. Never treat assets backing customer tokens as unrestricted corporate cash. |
| Shared US macro exposure | Verified CPI/PPI/Fed events and the relevant dated rate observations; issuer evidence for rate exposure. | A rate change can affect reserve earnings and equity discounting through different channels. State the conditions and avoid a single blanket direction for every stock. |

These rows are original research questions to be tested. They do not imply that
all requested metrics are public or that a source adapter already supports them.
Preserve missing values, incompatible measurement periods, incentives and source
methodology. Do not sum bridged representations into circulation or equate raw
transfer volume with payments, paying customers or recognized revenue.

## Example of an acceptable provisional assessment

For the Open USD announcement alone, a defensible output is: potential competitive
pressure on USDC distribution economics; evidence is insufficient to estimate
Circle's earnings or share-price impact. The hypothesis becomes stronger with
verified substitution or worsening disclosed distribution economics, and weaker
with evidence of retained usage and profitable growth despite competing rails.
Specify the assessment horizon and next review date when generating an actual
brief. This illustrates the output standard; it is not a current CRCL forecast.
