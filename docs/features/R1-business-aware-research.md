## Non-negotiable interpretation boundaries

- Numerical decomposition and metric validity are deterministic, versioned code.
  AI may later explain supported evidence, but never owns calculations, replaces
  unavailable input data, alters saved assumptions or silently changes a profile.
- Mathematical contributions do not establish business causes. Causes require
  filing/news evidence, source dates, alternatives/counterevidence and uncertainty.
  No-AI operation retains all deterministic explanations and review workflows.
- Profile applicability depends on economic model, lifecycle and instrument;
  sector/theme provides context. “Tech,” “tokenization” and “mining” are not a
  single operating model. Preserve signed loss/cash/debt/dilution facts even if
  a multiple is unsuitable. No automatic buy/sell or universal valuation score.
- F1's current no-runway exclusion remains. A future runway/funding or specialized
  metric must define cash availability, claims, period and assumptions under review.
- Preserve company 3/5/10-year options, original valuation work, sector graphs,
  source provenance, immutable history and the continuous N1 watchlist/macro scope.
- New shape/status/concept/provider policy is not approved by this prioritization.
  D021/D024 and the new refinement review agenda retain their existing boundaries.

## User review surfaces

states. A known unsupported profile is visible with required inputs, never a bad
score. A new earnings result is compared against the old expectation and evidence,
not used to retrospectively improve what the user said. Planned or mock outcomes
remain labelled. A prototype does not establish live-data or calculation acceptance.
