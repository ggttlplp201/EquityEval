## Implementation gaps shared by all seven

1. **Available source versus eligible result.** A browsed SEC document is discoverable evidence, not an archived application capture or successful calculation. Reuse `SecSource`, `LocalArchive`, `run_source_stages`, `publish_bundle`, the PIT reader and `FinancialInput`; see [S3 implementation](../design/s3/implementation.md). Every selected value still needs exact accession/period/scope/unit/precision, verified bytes, pinned mapping and authority revisions, and relevant quality/event evidence. Preserve `None` and flags.

2. **Mappings and vocabulary.** At the D1b baseline, `reviewed_cohort_rules` contains only CRCL's FY2025 anchor among these seven. It supplies no rule for the other six or the Q2 filings above. The 40-member [Concept enum](../../packages/schema/concepts.py) contains general statement concepts; it has no dedicated reserve-yield/distribution, bitcoin-holdings, trading-volume, customer-count, project-output, price-protection or segment-driver vocabulary. This is an application coverage gap, not proof those values are absent from SEC filings. Company Facts is not a substitute for missing custom/dimensional extraction. Do not add concepts or use unrelated existing ones inside the pilot presentation.

3. **Time, precision and histories.** Submissions/history completeness, filing amendments, scoped non-reliance events, reporting editions and original precision need explicit review. A browsed June statement is not a complete TTM series. Acquired/recast scope may make an otherwise available pair incomparable. Preserve 3/5/10-year choices and show actual missing/ineligible samples; do not shorten or fabricate history. Core ratio eligibility remains distinct from source-amount visibility.

4. **Prices and reference data.** None of this work supplies a live price, eligible exchange-session close, corporate-action history, current shares, complete issuer capitalization, reviewed sector taxonomy or historical membership. Require compatible dated instrument/reference records and all relevant common/senior claims. The seven selected companies are neither a whole sector nor one peer cohort. Existing [source permissions](../data-licences.md), S4b lifecycle work and S6 result contracts retain their recorded boundaries.

5. **Drivers and applicability.** The matrix and catalog describe review requirements. They do not authorize specialist models, runway, normalized business-driver storage, an automatic score or a buy/sell signal. Generic statement amounts may be retained while an interpretation is unsuitable. Mathematical attribution remains distinct from management explanations and causal hypotheses; D027's method and publication review still applies.
