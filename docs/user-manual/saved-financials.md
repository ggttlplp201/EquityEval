# Saved Financials

Open `/development/company/financials` from the Financials navigation link.
This is Fictional Statement Co., a separate saved example from Fundamentals,
Company overview and Sector Explorer. The page contains no real company data.

1. Choose **Annual** or **Quarterly**, then **Saved edition**. The annual example
   has an original and a revised 2025 filing. The quarter is directly reported
   2026 Q1; it is not derived from annual or overlapping YTD values.
2. Check **Filed / known on**, **Retrieved**, and **History mode**. The original
   edition contains only its own saved values. The revised edition labels changed
   values Restated and repeated values Unchanged. Selecting it does not replace
   the original snapshot. Latest reported means that saved revision only.
3. Use the Income statement, Balance sheet and Cash flow links. Income and cash
   flow are durations; balance sheet is an instant at period end. USD amounts are
   in units (scale 1), consolidated issuer scope, illustrative US GAAP basis.
4. Select any amount, including N/A, to open exact evidence. Escape closes the
   drawer and returns keyboard focus. The drawer includes original decimal text,
   fixture pointer/content hash, filed/known/retrieved dates, period and unit IDs,
   source/issuer/snapshot identity, identity transform and revision lineage.
5. Table values show at most four decimals. The source drawer retains every
   supplied decimal digit. A missing value is null with a quality flag; it is
   never zero. Coverage counts only the selected lines in each section. Select
   either count to inspect the supplied coverage record and the exact selected-row
   roster, including each row’s source evidence and the saved snapshot identity.

**Income statement**: revenues and expenses over a period. **Gross profit**:
revenue less related cost, if supplied as a reported line. **Operating income**:
profit from operations. **Net income**: profit after other expenses and taxes.
**Balance sheet**: assets, liabilities and equity at a date. **Cash flow**: cash
movements over a period. **PPE**: property, plant and equipment. **NCI**:
noncontrolling interests. **Restatement**: a later source changes an earlier
reported value. **Known on**: fictional evidence-availability date, separate from
financial period and filing date. **Retrieved**: when the source was captured.

TTM is disabled: no reviewed twelve-month statement export is supplied. No rows
are summed, inferred or zero-filled. These selected lines are not full financial
statements and do not assert all accounting reconciliation checks passed. Real
publication, fuller statement coverage and derived-period assembly remain later
work. Existing Fundamentals 3/5/10-year comparisons are unchanged.
