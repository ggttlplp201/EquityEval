# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An individual researching stocks locally, moving from a five-minute review into
source evidence, assumptions and comparison history. The current application has
fictional Sector and Company views, a News configuration/coverage workspace,
a searchable Help guide and a production-readiness page.

## Product Purpose

Make financial facts, model limits and explicit human reasoning inspectable.
Preserve the 143-item design checklist and the seven-stock pilot: CRCL, MSTR,
COIN, HOOD, USAR, MP, GOOGL. Source work and current core calculations are reused.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, evidence-led. The owner supplied the dark Sector Explorer redesign
on 2026-09-19 and authorized resuming the build. Its charcoal surfaces, mint chart
marks and compact Helvetica typography define the visual direction.

## Anti-references

No ornamental dashboard cards, universal stock scores, buy/sell badges, invented
live data, or implied active monitoring. No frontend financial calculation layer.

## Design Principles

- Graphs lead, with tables and evidence always reachable.
- Separate aggregate ratios from typical-company ratios and disclose eligibility.
- Keep missingness, limited coverage and unsupported interpretations explicit.
- Facts, assumptions and conclusions remain distinct; saved history is immutable.
- Reuse completed work; show later research and monitoring scope as planned.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, labelled charts with table alternatives,
readable small text, mobile-contained table scrolling and reduced-motion support.
Distinguish chart series with pattern as well as color/opacity. The design's dim
opacity-only histories are adapted where required for contrast and identification.
